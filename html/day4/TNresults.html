About the TN Election

The Tamil Nadu Election Result 2026 Portal is a trusted platform created to provide live election updates, constituency-wise results, party-wise seat counts, and the latest political news across the state. Our website helps citizens easily access accurate and real-time election information in a simple and user-friendly manner. Users can quickly check winning candidates, vote counting status, alliance performance, district-wise results, and overall election statistics without any difficulty. The portal is designed to deliver fast updates during the counting process so that people can stay informed every minute. Tamil Nadu elections are one of the most important democratic events in the state, attracting attention from millions of voters and political followers. Our mission is to provide transparent, reliable, and official election information for students, voters, media viewers, and the general public. We continuously work to ensure smooth access to election data with clear presentation and easy navigation. Through this platform, users can stay connected with the latest developments, political trends, and final election outcomes from all constituencies of Tamil Nadu.

result

According to recent 2026 election result reports, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) led by Vijay emerged as the biggest party in the Tamil Nadu Assembly election. Approximate seat results (234 total seats): Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) — around 108 seats Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK alliance) — around 73 seats All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK alliance) — around 53 seats Important points: TVK became the single largest party but was slightly below the majority mark of 118 seats. M. K. Stalin and DMK faced major losses. This election was considered historic because it broke the long-time DMK vs AIADMK dominance in Tamil Nadu politics.
